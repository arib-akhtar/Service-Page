Privacy Commitment: At Aivon Tech, we secure and value your personal data. Click on any section below to expand and read our data handling rules.

We collect the following types of information in the course of providing our services: 2.1 Information You Provide Directly • Discovery Form Data: Business name, contact details, website goals, target audience, service requirements, and current operational bottlenecks — collected via Google Forms. • Asset Handover Files: Logo files, raw media, brand guidelines, credentials, and reference materials — shared via Google Sheets and secure file transfer. • WhatsApp Messages: Text messages, media files, voice notes, and documents shared through our WhatsApp Business line. • Email Communications: Correspondence sent to help@aivontech.com, support@aivontech.com, or other designated email addresses. • Payment Information: Transaction details processed through standard payment methods (UPI, bank transfer). We use collected information for the following purposes: • Service Delivery: To fulfill your service requests, create deliverables, and manage project workflows. • Communication: To respond to inquiries, provide project updates, and send important notifications via WhatsApp and email. • Project Management: To track deliverables, manage revisions, and maintain engagement records via Google Sheets and WhatsApp. • Service Improvement: To analyze aggregated, anonymized data for improving our service quality and processes. • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, and legal obligations.
We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data: • Google Workspace: Discovery forms and asset tracking sheets are hosted on Google Workspace with enterprise-grade security, encryption at rest, and access controls. • WhatsApp Business: All WhatsApp communications are protected by end-to-end encryption provided by the WhatsApp platform. • Web Hosting: Our website is hosted on secure servers with SSL/TLS encryption, regular security patches, and automated backups. • Access Control: Only authorized Aivon Tech team members have access to client data, governed by internal confidentiality agreements. • No Data Sales: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing or any other purpose.

• Essential Cookies: Required for basic website functionality (e.g., navigation, session management). These cannot be disabled. • Analytics Cookies: Optional cookies used to understand how visitors interact with our website. Data is anonymized and aggregated. • No Invasive Tracking: We do not use cross-site tracking, fingerprinting, or invasive surveillance technologies. • Consent: Analytics cookies are only activated with your explicit consent.
